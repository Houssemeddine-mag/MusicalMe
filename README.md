# 🌟 Tech Graden

### *Where little hands make big magic.*

Tech Graden is a small world of play for children, built around the only controller they
already own: their hands. ✋ There's no mouse to learn, no keyboard to reach for, no screen to
tap. A child sits in front of a webcam, lifts a hand, and the room responds.

Three rooms wait inside. In **MusicalMe** 🎹, touching a thumb to a finger plays a note, and
raising a hand lifts the pitch. In **Mathic** ➗, numbers can be built, solved, and heard out
loud in English and French. In **YouDraw** 🎨, two fingers held together become a paintbrush,
and a closed fist becomes an eraser.

Everything opens from one bright, friendly screen — the Hub 🏠 — where each room is a big
picture. Point at it, pinch, and you're there. Point at the door, pinch, and you're back.

---

## ✨ A moment worth building for

A child raises a hand, touches their thumb to a finger, and a note rings out. 🎵 They move
their hand higher and the sound climbs with it. They open both hands and suddenly they're
playing a chord.

That pause — the wide eyes, the small giggle, the *"I did that"* — is the whole reason this
exists. Tech Graden isn't trying to teach a child to use software. It's trying to give them a
place where their own movement makes something beautiful happen. 💛

## 🌈 The rooms inside

### 🎹 MusicalMe — *make some noise*

The music room, and usually the first place a child goes. Eight notes are spread across both
hands, so chords arrive almost by accident. Four instruments — piano, guitar, synthesizer,
flute — change the whole mood of the room, and four color themes let a child make it theirs.

### ➗ Mathic — *numbers that play back*

The quiet, thoughtful room. Some children will want to build an equation and watch it shrink
away as the answer appears in green. 🟢 Others will want the quiz, with its four gentle levels
and its forgiving *"ooh, try again."* And some will simply want to meet the numbers, one at a
time, and hear them spoken in two languages. 🇬🇧 🇫🇷

### 🎨 YouDraw — *draw with the air*

The one that needs no instructions at all. Pick a color, pick a brush, and draw in the air.
👆 Point with one finger to choose something new, keep two fingers together to paint, close
your fist to erase. ✊ Fast swooshes stay smooth, which matters more than you'd think.

### 🏠 The Hub — *a home base*

A bright launch screen where every activity is a big, friendly picture. Hover and it grows.
Pinch and it opens. The camera stays warm the whole time, so hopping from music to math to
drawing feels instant — no waiting, no lost momentum. ⚡

## 🚀 Getting started

On Windows, it takes two double-clicks.

1️⃣ First run **`setup_venv.bat`**. It sets everything up in its own little corner of the
computer, so it won't disturb anything else you have installed. This is the slow part — a few
minutes, worth a cup of tea. ☕

2️⃣ Then run **`run.bat`**. The Hub appears, and you're off. 🎉

You'll need a webcam 📷, a speaker or headphones 🎧, and a little room to wave your arms.

## 🖐️ The gestures

They take about a minute to learn, and then they become instinct.

| What you do | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 👆 Point with your index finger | Your cursor follows |
| 🤏 Touch thumb and index together | Select, click, choose |
| 🤙 Raise just your pinky | Open or close the menu |
| ✋ Touch thumb to different fingers | Play different notes |
| ↕️ Move your hand up or down | Change the pitch |
| 🙌 Use both hands | Play chords |
| ✊ Close your fist, in YouDraw | Erase |
| ⌨️ Press Q | Head back to the Hub |

In MusicalMe, the notes are laid out like this:

| Left hand | Note | Right hand | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thumb + Pinky | **DO** | Thumb + Index | **SOL** |
| Thumb + Ring | **RE** | Thumb + Middle | **LA** |
| Thumb + Middle | **MI** | Thumb + Ring | **SI** |
| Thumb + Index | **FA** | Thumb + Pinky | **DO**, higher |

## 💛 Why it feels good to use

Not every child takes to a mouse, and not every child should have to. Hands are the most
natural input there is, and the gestures here are deliberately large and forgiving. Small
hands aren't precise — they shouldn't need to be. 👐

Every action answers back immediately. A note sounds the moment fingers meet. A picture grows
when you hover over it. A correct answer turns green. That quickness is what keeps a child
curious instead of frustrated. ✨

And because music, numbers, and drawing all live in the same place, interests can wander. A
child who lights up at sound might drift into counting. A child who only wanted to draw might
stay for the melody. 🎶

It's also calm in the ways that matter. No accounts, no advertisements, no internet
connection. Nothing a child does ever leaves the room. 🔒

## 🌱 What's coming next

The Hub already shows three more doors — 🅰️ Alphabet, 🦁 TheZoo, and 🚗 CarRoad — waiting to
be opened. Beyond those, we're hoping for follow-the-melody games, deeper math with gentle
progress tracking, more languages 🌍, spoken cues for every button 🎤, steadier pointing, and
an install that asks nothing of a family beyond a single click 📦.

We'd also like every child to be able to join in: larger text, higher contrast, less motion,
and a keyboard for anyone who'd rather not use a camera. ♿
